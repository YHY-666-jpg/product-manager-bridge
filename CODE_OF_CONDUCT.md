# Code of Conduct

Product Manager Bridge follows the Contributor Covenant principles:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but take impact seriously.
- Keep discussions focused on the work.
- Do not harass, threaten, or demean other contributors.
- Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributors that violate these standards.
